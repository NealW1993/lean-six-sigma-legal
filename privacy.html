Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

This policy explains how the Six Sigma mobile app stores, transmits, shares, protects, and deletes information.

Privacy request

01 Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to the Six Sigma mobile app (the "App"). The publisher identified in the App's store listing is responsible for the App. Questions, privacy requests, or support requests may be sent to nealwiechman@gmail.com.

Six Sigma is primarily an offline productivity and analysis tool. It does not require a publisher-hosted username and password account. Optional cloud, AI, shared-workspace, and purchase features transmit limited information as described below.

02 Information stored on your device

The App may store the following in app-private local storage:

  • Projects, workspaces, notes, meetings, comments, AI outputs, charts, calculations, analyses, data sets, examples, and preferences.
  • Documents, images, audio recordings, exports, and other files that you import, capture, record, or create.
  • A display name, selected industry, belt level, interface preferences, and local sync status.
  • A small, redacted diagnostic history when the App encounters an error.

API keys, database credentials, workspace passphrase verifiers, and Supabase authentication refresh tokens are stored separately using Android secure storage or Apple Keychain. Secret credential values are excluded from JSON exports and work-data backups.

03 Optional cloud and shared-workspace connections

When you configure a Personal or Shared Connection, the App sends the selected work data to the Supabase project or compatible HTTPS endpoint that you or your organization provides. This can include projects, workspaces, notes, meetings, comments, charts, calculations, analyses, data sets, notifications, permissions, and related metadata.

Supabase may assign an anonymous authentication identity to the App installation. That identity and database row-level security are used to authorize a personal row or shared-workspace membership. Shared content is visible to members according to workspace and item-level permissions. The database owner controls the connected database, its geographic location, retention, logs, backups, and member access.

If a shared database is temporarily unavailable and you choose to continue, pending work may be stored locally until the connection is restored. The App does not operate a default publisher-hosted cloud account for your work data.

04 Optional AI providers

AI is off unless you configure and enable it. When you request an AI output, the App sends your prompt and any project context you select directly to the provider you configured, such as OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, xAI, or a custom compatible endpoint. The provider processes that information under its own privacy policy and terms. Do not submit confidential, regulated, or personal data unless you are authorized to do so and accept the provider's data practices.

05 Purchases and entitlement verification

Google Play or Apple's App Store processes Pro Unlock payments. The App uses RevenueCat to verify, restore, and revoke the entitlement. RevenueCat receives an anonymous App user identifier, store, product, transaction, and entitlement information and may provide purchase-history dashboard analytics. The App does not receive your complete payment-card number.

Automatic device identifier collection and RevenueCat diagnostics are disabled in the App's RevenueCat configuration. Store providers may independently process device, account, payment, fraud-prevention, and diagnostic information under their own policies.

06 Device permissions

  • Camera: used only for user-initiated data capture, document capture, invite scanning, or credential scanning.
  • Microphone: used only for user-initiated audio notes and meeting recordings.
  • Notifications: used for reminders, sync status, and shared-workspace activity.
  • Files and media: used for documents, attachments, exports, and backup locations you select.

You may control these permissions through your device settings.

07 How information is shared

The App shares or transmits information only as needed for:

  • Services that you configure, including your selected database and AI provider.
  • Google Play, Apple's App Store, and RevenueCat for purchase processing and entitlement management.
  • Workspace members with whom you intentionally share content.
  • Exports, email drafts, print jobs, or operating-system share actions that you initiate.
  • Compliance with applicable law or protection of rights and safety when legally required.

The App has no advertising SDK, does not sell personal information, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for targeted advertising.

08 Retention, deletion, and control

Settings > Local Data allows you to delete local projects, notes, meetings, charts, calculations, analyses, data sets, imported files, media, diagnostics, and protected credentials. You can also remove the App to delete app-private local data, subject to operating-system backup behavior and files you exported elsewhere.

The App does not provide a publisher-hosted account because no such account is required. Cloud copies are controlled by the owner of the database you connected. Requests concerning data in a third-party or organization-owned database should be directed to that database owner. Purchase records remain subject to store and RevenueCat legal and accounting retention requirements.

09 Local diagnostics

Runtime errors may be retained in a count- and size-limited history in app-private storage. The App redacts values resembling credentials, email addresses, URL query parameters, and user home paths. Diagnostics are not uploaded automatically. You decide whether to review, copy, export, clear, or send a report to support.

10 Security

The App uses platform-protected credential storage, HTTPS, authenticated requests, and database row-level security where configured. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting provider credentials, invitation passphrases, exported files, unlocked devices, and the databases you connect.

11 Children

Six Sigma is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. The publisher does not knowingly solicit personal information from children through the App.

12 International processing and your rights

Services you choose may process information in countries other than your own. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. Because most work data remains local or is sent to services you configure, use the App's deletion controls and contact the relevant service or database owner first. You may also contact the publisher at nealwiechman@gmail.com.

13 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App, its providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or store listing where appropriate.
