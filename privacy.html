Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

This policy explains how the Six Sigma mobile and Windows apps store, transmit, share, protect, and delete information.

Privacy request

01 Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy applies to supported mobile and Windows desktop versions of the Six Sigma app (the "App"). The publisher identified in the App's store listing or distribution page is responsible for the App. Questions, privacy requests, or support requests may be sent to nealwiechman@gmail.com.

Six Sigma is primarily an offline productivity and analysis tool. It does not require a publisher-hosted username and password account. The App may create pseudonymous authentication identities to secure optional database connections, verify Pro Unlock, and link authorized devices. Optional cloud, AI, shared-workspace, cross-device, and purchase features transmit information as described below.

02 Information stored on your device

The App may store the following in app-private local storage:

  • Projects, workspaces, notes, meetings, comments, AI outputs, charts, calculations, analyses, data sets, examples, and preferences.
  • Documents, images, audio recordings, exports, and other files that you import, capture, record, or create.
  • A display name, selected industry, belt level, interface preferences, and local sync status.
  • A small, redacted diagnostic history when the App encounters an error.

API keys, database credentials, workspace passphrase verifiers, pairing credentials, and Supabase authentication refresh tokens are stored separately using Android secure storage, Apple Keychain, or Windows Credential Manager, as applicable. Secret credential values are excluded from JSON exports and work-data backups. Files saved to an external backup folder or another location you select remain outside app-private storage and are controlled by you.

03 Optional cloud and shared-workspace connections

When you configure a Personal or Shared Connection, the App sends the selected work data to the Supabase project or compatible HTTPS endpoint that you or your organization provides. This can include projects, workspaces, notes, meetings, comments, charts, calculations, analyses, data sets, activity notifications, permissions, file-transfer records, and related metadata. Private attachment or media bytes may also be uploaded when you enable transfer between authorized devices.

Supabase may assign a separate pseudonymous authentication identity to each App installation. Authentication identifiers, device or member display names, roles, membership status, and activity times may be stored to authorize a personal vault or shared-workspace membership. Database row-level security limits access according to those memberships and item-level permissions. The database owner controls the connected database, its geographic location, retention, logs, backups, and member access.

If a shared database is temporarily unavailable and you choose to continue, pending work may be stored locally until the connection is restored. Your projects, notes, charts, calculations, media, and other work data are not sent to the publisher's entitlement service; they remain local or are sent to the database you or your organization configured.

04 Paired devices and cross-device synchronization

A Pro mobile installation may create a time-limited, one-time invitation for an authorized Windows device. The invitation is encrypted with a passphrase you choose and may contain the selected Supabase endpoint, publishable key, personal-vault identifier, one-time pairing capabilities, and an owner label. The App displays the invitation as a QR code or encrypted text only when you request it. Anyone who receives both the invitation and passphrase may try to redeem it before it expires, so send them separately and only to a device you control.

A paired device receives its own Supabase authentication identity; the mobile device's refresh token, store credential, and database secret are not copied to Windows. The user-controlled Personal Connection may store the paired device's name, role, membership, last activity, invitation-token hash, and revocation status. After pairing, authorized records can synchronize through that database without a direct phone-to-PC connection.

Device-local file paths do not synchronize. When private file transfer is enabled, the App may upload file bytes and metadata needed to identify, verify, and download the file through private storage in the configured Supabase project. Until downloaded, the other device may show a placeholder. In-App activity records may synchronize, but operating-system notification history and read state remain device-specific.

Synchronization may combine records created on different devices. For competing edits to the same record, the App generally keeps the newest recognized edit; deletions may be represented by a deletion marker that propagates later. Revoking a paired device stops future authorized access after verification, but it cannot erase copies, exports, backups, or media already stored on that device.

The publisher-operated entitlement service stores only the pseudonymous information needed to verify and link Pro access. This may include a random authentication identifier used as a RevenueCat App User ID, entitlement and product status, store and environment, verification times, linked owner and device identifiers, a device label and platform, link or revocation times, and one-time invitation hashes and expirations. Hosting providers may also process IP addresses and request logs as part of operating and securing the service. Windows periodically contacts this service to verify Pro access and may temporarily use a recent cached verification while offline.

05 Optional AI providers

AI is off unless you configure and enable it. When you request an AI output, the App sends your prompt and any project context you select directly to the provider you configured, such as OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, xAI, or a custom compatible endpoint. The provider processes that information under its own privacy policy and terms. Do not submit confidential, regulated, or personal data unless you are authorized to do so and accept the provider's data practices.

06 Purchases and entitlement verification

Google Play or Apple's App Store processes Pro Unlock payments on a supported mobile device. The App uses RevenueCat to verify, restore, and revoke the entitlement. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous App User ID, store, product, transaction, and entitlement information and may provide purchase-history dashboard analytics. The App does not receive your complete payment-card number.

Automatic device identifier collection and RevenueCat diagnostics are disabled in the App's RevenueCat configuration. Store providers may independently process device, account, payment, fraud-prevention, and diagnostic information under their own policies.

Windows does not process the mobile store purchase. An authorized Windows installation checks the publisher entitlement service to confirm that it remains linked to an active mobile Pro entitlement.

07 Device permissions and hardware

  • Camera or webcam: used only for user-initiated data capture, document capture, invite scanning, or credential scanning.
  • Microphone: used only for user-initiated audio notes and meeting recordings when compatible hardware is available.
  • Notifications: used for reminders, sync status, and shared-workspace activity.
  • Files and media: used for documents, attachments, exports, downloads, and backup locations you select.

The App does not intentionally record camera or microphone input in the background. Hardware availability and permission controls vary by operating system, and you may change permissions through the applicable device settings.

08 How information is shared

The App shares or transmits information only as needed for:

  • Services that you configure, including your selected database and AI provider.
  • Google Play, Apple's App Store, RevenueCat, and the publisher entitlement service for purchase and paired-device entitlement management.
  • Workspace members with whom you intentionally share content.
  • Paired devices that you authorize to access a Personal Connection or private file transfer.
  • Exports, email drafts, print jobs, or operating-system share actions that you initiate.
  • Compliance with applicable law or protection of rights and safety when legally required.

The App has no advertising SDK, does not sell personal information, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for targeted advertising.

09 Retention, deletion, and control

Settings > Local Data allows you to delete local projects, notes, meetings, charts, calculations, analyses, data sets, imported files, media, diagnostics, and protected credentials. You can also remove the App to delete app-private local data, subject to operating-system backup behavior. Uninstalling the App does not remove exports, selected backup folders, files downloaded elsewhere, or remote database copies.

Synced edits and deletions may propagate to other authorized devices, but propagation requires a working connection. Cloud copies are controlled by the owner of the database you connected. Removing a connection or revoking a member or paired device does not automatically delete copies already downloaded, exported, or backed up. Requests concerning data in a third-party or organization-owned database should be directed to that database owner.

Publisher entitlement and paired-license records are retained as needed to verify purchases, prevent abuse, provide support, honor revocation, and satisfy legal or accounting requirements. Purchase records remain subject to store and RevenueCat retention rules. You may request deletion of publisher-controlled pseudonymous device or entitlement-link data by contacting the email above, subject to records the publisher must retain. Such a request does not delete work data held in a database controlled by you or your organization.

10 Local diagnostics

Runtime errors may be retained in a count- and size-limited history in app-private storage. The App redacts values resembling credentials, email addresses, URL query parameters, and user home paths. Diagnostics are not uploaded automatically. You decide whether to review, copy, export, clear, or send a report to support.

11 Security

The App uses platform-protected credential storage, HTTPS, authenticated requests, and database row-level security where configured. Pairing and workspace invitations use one-time capabilities, expiration, and passphrase protection where provided. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting provider credentials, invitation passphrases, exported files, unlocked devices, and the databases you connect, and for revoking devices that are lost or no longer trusted.

12 Children

Six Sigma is a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. The publisher does not knowingly solicit personal information from children through the App.

13 International processing and your rights

Services you choose may process information in countries other than your own. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. Because most work data remains local or is sent to services you configure, use the App's deletion controls and contact the relevant service or database owner first. For publisher-controlled entitlement or paired-license records, you may contact the publisher at nealwiechman@gmail.com.

14 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App, its providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or store listing where appropriate.
